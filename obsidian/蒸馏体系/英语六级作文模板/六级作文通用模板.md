# 英语六级作文通用模板（简化词汇版）

## 模板特点
- 替换原模板中的生僻词为简单词
- 参照近年真题（社交媒体/AI/就业/中国梦/教师影响/竞争）提炼通用性
- 每篇 150-200 词，首句必须抄给定句子

---

## 一、四段式通用模板

### Para 1 — 引出话题（首句照抄 + 过渡）

> **[抄写题目给出的首句]** Actually, [主题词] plays a big role in our daily lives. Many students think it has nothing to do with them, but looking ahead, we cannot just ignore it.

### Para 2 — 正面论证（理由1 + 理由2）

> One reason why [主题词] matters is that it helps us learn new things and build useful skills. For example, it can make us better at thinking and solving problems. Another reason is that it brings us real benefits, like getting helpful information quickly or making our life richer and more colorful.

### Para 3 — 个人经历/举例

> Take myself as an example. By using / getting involved in / learning about [主题词], I have improved a lot in my study / work / daily life. This experience has shown me how important it really is.

### Para 4 — 总结

> In short, spending time on [主题词] is necessary for our future success, both at school and at work. We should take it seriously and make good use of it.

## 四、万能句式速记

**开头过渡**：Actually, [主题] plays a big role in our daily lives.

**理由1**：One reason why [主题] matters is that it helps us learn new things and build useful skills.

**理由2**：Another reason is that it brings us real benefits, like getting helpful information quickly or making our life richer and more colorful.

**举例**：Take myself as an example. By [动词ing + 主题], I have improved a lot in my study / work / daily life.

**总结**：In short, spending time on [主题] is necessary for our future success, both at school and at work.
